# RESEARCH profile

## Correctness

Conclusions need confidence proportional to evidence: source quality, coverage,
contradiction handling, and calibrated uncertainty. Label material claims
`FACT`, `INFERENCE`, `HYPOTHESIS`, or `SPECULATION` when distinction matters;
never silently collapse them. Separate source statements from synthesis and
retain provenance for re-evaluation.

## Procedure

Define the question; gather and assess evidence quality, provenance, and
recency; form and challenge conclusions; synthesize uncertainty. Evidence needs
mean: `NONE` stable knowledge; `INTERNAL` supplied/repository/runtime evidence;
`EXTERNAL` current sources; `PRIMARY` original/authoritative sources;
`MULTI_SOURCE` independent coverage or competing views. Prefer primary sources
for important facts. Start refreshes from canonical state and retrieve targeted
evidence before full sources; see [Core context](../../core/context.md).

Time-sensitive facts, predictions, credible conflict, contested topics,
important decisions, unclear provenance, inference gaps, or missing primary
evidence escalate uncertainty or assurance.

- **L0:** stable fact/explanation without meaningful evidence burden.
- **L1:** sanity check and proportionate support.
- **L2:** structured collection, evaluation, synthesis, explicit uncertainty.
- **L3:** independent challenge, primary verification, contradiction search, or independent sources.
- **L4:** rare high-impact checkpoints, multiple evidence types, decision criteria, and human review where appropriate.

An L3 independent path may be alternative evidence rather than another agent.

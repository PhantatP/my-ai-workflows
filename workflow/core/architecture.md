# Conceptual architecture

Keep these concerns separate:

1. **Core Router** sets process and assurance.
2. **Domain Profile** defines BUILD, RESEARCH, or LEARN correctness.
3. **Execution Strategy** selects mechanisms.
4. **Model Router** selects capability.
5. **Adapter** maps policy to platform capability.

Assistant operations support domains; they are not a fourth correctness domain.
Information policy supplies cross-domain artifact roles, lifecycles, and
provenance. Mixed-task segments may have different levels; Main integrates them.

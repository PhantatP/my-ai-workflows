# V2.2 core principles

1. Main owns the task but is not sole authority over minimum rigor.
2. Use the least orchestration that supplies sufficient reliability.
3. Complexity determines coordination needs; risk determines verification
   needs.
4. Mechanical facts and judgment risks are separate inputs.
5. Mechanical triggers establish a minimum level. Main may escalate, never
   narratively override the floor.
6. A mechanical floor may be removed only by inspectable evidence recorded as
   a trigger-disproof event.
7. Before initial Levels 0–2 work, and before every de-escalation, state a
   possible miss and the concrete evidence that would force escalation.
8. New evidence always re-enters the routing process. Sensitive files entering
   the *actual* change set automatically reapply mechanical policy.
9. An agent supplies independent context or judgment. A skill supplies a
   reusable methodology. A phase does not automatically require an agent.
10. Verification is part of implementation. Independent review is an attempt
    to falsify correctness, not a request for approval.
11. Mechanical events are appended to the human-readable operational log;
    reasons, risks, findings, and remaining uncertainty are explicitly semantic.
12. BUILD is the current operational core. RESEARCH and LEARN are experimental.
